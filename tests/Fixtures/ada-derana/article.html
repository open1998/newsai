The U.S. military has enough assets to maintain a naval blockade of Iran indefinitely, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said on Thursday.

 

"Indefinitely the United States Navy can maintain a blockade like that because we'll rotate ships in and out," Hegseth told reporters.

He spoke in Panama after addressing the crew aboard the USS Gridley, a guided-missile destroyer.
